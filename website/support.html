How can we help?

For app help, account questions, privacy requests or a safety concern, contact Gara’s operator, Daniel Roci.

gara.support@gmail.com

Getting started

Already have access to Gara on iPhone? Create a verified account without an invite. Use verified email, Sign in with Apple or Google where offered. Gara is for adults 18+. A public App Store download will be linked from the homepage when its listing is live.

App or account help

Describe what happened and include your iPhone model and app version if available. Never send a password, sign-in code or unnecessary location history.

Delete your account

Profile → Settings → Account → Delete account

Open your profile from the header avatar, then follow the path above. Confirm your identity when prompted. Apple-linked accounts also need Apple confirmation and successful authorization revocation. If a step fails, retry or email us; do not assume the account was deleted.

Successful deletion removes your account and linked profile-owned server records, then attempts to clear account-related local data, including automatic tracks. Separately justified report evidence, provider backups and copies outside Gara may remain as described in the Privacy Policy. If you cannot sign in, email a deletion request so we can verify account ownership. Uninstalling the app alone does not delete your account.

Your private Driving Map

Automatic drive mapping is optional and off by default. Turn it off or erase locally saved automatic tracks in Settings. Changing iPhone location permissions stops future permission-dependent access but does not itself erase saved records. Automatic tracks do not currently sync to another device. Saved route drives are separate account records.

Privacy requests

You can email an access, correction or deletion request. Include enough information to identify the relevant account; we may need to verify that it belongs to you. Do not send identity documents unless specifically requested through a suitable secure process.

Content and safety

Use Report on a post, profile or comment to send the selected content and your reason to Gara’s restricted review queue. Use Block on a driver’s profile to restrict affected contact and visibility in both directions. Blocking removes follow/friend relationships; unblocking does not restore them. You can manage blocked drivers in Settings. Direct Messages is not included in version 1.0.

For an unsafe or inappropriate route, use Report route by email in Route Details if your version has it. You can also email the route name, its creator if known, and a brief explanation. Email us if an in-app report fails or to ask us to review a moderation decision. Leave out unrelated conversations and location history.

Gara is not an emergency service. Contact local emergency services for immediate danger. Do not use the app while driving.

Automatic filtering is currently off for real submissions. When enabled, the app asks permission before sending the text or photo being checked to OpenAI. Reports and support email remain available independently of that check. Gara is operated by one person; there is no guaranteed immediate response.
