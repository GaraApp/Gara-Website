How can we help?

For app help, account questions, privacy requests or a safety concern, contact Gara’s operator, Daniel Roci.

gara.support@gmail.com

Secure-account transition

Gara is preparing secure launch accounts. Old prototype login codes no longer work. Email delivery has been tested, but public sign-in remains unavailable while end-to-end account and release checks are completed.

App or account help

Describe what happened and include your iPhone model and app version if available. Never send a password, sign-in code or unnecessary location history.

Privacy requests

You can email an access, correction or deletion request. Include enough information to identify the relevant account; we may need to verify that it belongs to you. Do not send identity documents unless specifically requested through a suitable secure process.

Content and safety

Report concerning content from the app’s safety controls when available. If those controls are unavailable, email the content reference and a brief description. Do not include unrelated private conversations or another person’s location.

Gara is not an emergency service. Contact local emergency services for immediate danger. Do not use the app while driving.
