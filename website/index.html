How do I join Gara?

If you already have access to the iPhone app, you can create a verified account without an invite. Use verified email, Sign in with Apple or Google where offered. Gara is for adults aged 18 and over. The public App Store download will be linked here when its listing is live.

What can other people see?

Your profile, garage and shared content follow Gara’s visibility rules. Following and accepted friendship are different. Your raw GPS history from manually recorded route drives is not shown through ordinary access to your profile. Photos and routes you share can still reveal places. See the Privacy Policy.

Can I message other drivers?

Direct Messages is not part of version 1.0. Connect through profiles, follows, accepted friendships, posts and comments.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open Profile → Settings → Account → Delete account. You’ll be asked to confirm your identity. If you cannot sign in, contact Support. Deleting the app alone does not delete your account.
