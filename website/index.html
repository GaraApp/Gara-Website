The Gara experience

More to a drive
than the destination.

A place for the road ahead, the roads behind you, and everything you love in between.

Explore on GaraMap, find a route shared with you, or create one of your own. Follow a fixed path—or choose an open route and find your own way between its start and finish.

  • Preview the route before you begin
  • Browse yours, friends’ routes, or nearby
  • Choose a drive deliberately

Your recorded journeys become a private driving atlas. Familiar roads gain a warm glow as you return, while date filters help you look back at where you’ve been.

  • Personal history, separate from route results
  • Heat and street-name controls
  • Optional automatic mapping, saved on your iPhone

Automatic mapping needs permission and may miss journeys. Heat is a visual summary, rather than an exact road-completion measure.

A good road, a car detail, a moment worth keeping. Share photos, thoughts, and routes through a feed built around the people and roads you care about.

  • For you, Following, and Friends views
  • Photo, text, and route posts
  • Comments, saves, and shared context

Thought through, from the start

Your pace.
Your choices.

The road comes first. Gara is being built to make exploration feel personal, deliberate, and easy to understand.

01 / Control

Choose what you share.

Your raw recorded driving history is private. Routes and social content follow their audience rules, with following and friendship kept distinct.

02 / Clarity

Know what starts a drive.

Recommendations open for you to review. You decide when to start a route. Automatic everyday mapping is a separate, optional setting.

03 / Responsibility

Keep the road above the result.

Public-road speed and fastest-time competition are unavailable. Follow traffic laws, conditions, and closures; use the app while safely parked.

Can I download Gara yet?+

Gara is being prepared for iPhone testing. Public registration is not open and this website does not offer an App Store download yet. Contact us with questions about future access.

What’s the difference between a fixed and open route?+

A fixed route has a defined path. An open route has a start and finish, with the path between them left to you. Open routes do not provide directions. Always choose legal, suitable roads and respect closures.

Can other people see my driving map?+

Your raw recorded drive history and private driving map are not shown to other drivers through ordinary profile access. Shared routes, photos, and posts can still reveal places. Read the Privacy Policy for details.

Does Gara record every drive automatically?+

Everyday automatic mapping is optional and needs location permission. Its tracks stay on your iPhone, and some journeys may be missed. Deliberately recorded route drives are a separate feature.

Who is Gara for?+

People aged 18 or over who enjoy roads, cars, and sharing that interest with their circle. Driving eligibility and road-use laws still apply wherever you are.
