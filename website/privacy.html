Privacy Policy

Draft for Daniel’s review — not yet effective

Prepared 27 September 2026 from the current implementation. Unresolved items are labelled below. This is not a final launch policy or a claim of legal compliance.

Gara is operated by Daniel Roci, Sweden, responsible for the personal information described here. Contact gara.support@gmail.com for privacy questions and requests.

Gara is in a secure-account transition. Development testing supports verified email, Apple and Google sign-in, but public registration remains closed. Older screenshots and prototype data do not describe all current launch functionality. Chat and performance screens are temporarily hidden, although their data and code remain.

1. Information and its uses

  • Account: email, verified authentication identity, account identifiers and sign-in/session information are used to authenticate you and associate records with your account. Apple or Google sign-in, when offered and chosen, supplies provider identity information and an email address; Apple may supply a private relay address. Gara does not ask for your Apple or Google password.
  • Profile and garage: display name, username, chosen profile photo, vehicle details and images, and preferences such as language, measurement units and map style support your profile and app experience.
  • Social information: posts, captions, photos, comments, likes, follows, friendship requests and relationships support the social feed. Existing messaging records include participants, message text and read status. Notifications include activity references and read status. Hidden screens are not a claim that their existing records have been erased.
  • Routes and drives: route names, descriptions, surface, location, coordinates, distance, creator and audience support route discovery and navigation. Saved drives contain the route reference, date, duration, calculated speed statistics and GPS samples. These support personal history and the driving map, not public-road speed competition.
  • Support and safety: support emails, report reasons, content references and copies of reported text/photos/profile or message information support requests and abuse investigation. Blocking records support contact and visibility restrictions.

Session information, preferences and some saved items or cached data also remain on your device. This is not a promise that all local data is encrypted or stored in Keychain. Photo selection is initiated by a user action; only share images you have the right to use.

2. GPS and your profile driving map

With device location permission, Gara uses foreground location for the map, nearby discovery and navigation. When you choose to record and save a drive, samples can include precise latitude/longitude, timestamp, accuracy, speed and heading. Saved samples generate your own profile driving map; it is not automatic all-day tracking. The inspected implementation does not record drives through an always-on background tracking service.

The owner’s driving map uses saved drive traces. Another driver’s profile does not receive your raw GPS trace or this private driving history. Display limits and date filters do not delete the underlying saved drives.

You can change location or photo permissions in iPhone Settings. Denying location limits location-dependent features. Revoking permission stops future permission-dependent access; it does not delete saved records. Shared routes, route images and photos can still reveal places you visit, even when your raw trace is private.

3. Who can see information

Signed-in, non-blocked users can view profile and garage information under the current access rules. Social posts and routes have audience rules: public means visible to other authorized signed-in users, friends means accepted Gara friendships, and private routes are creator-only. Following is directional and does not grant friendship access. Newly saved routes currently default to friends; do not assume every supported audience can be selected in every screen.

Raw recorded drive GPS is owner-only under ordinary app access. Messages are restricted to their participants, subject to blocking. Reported content may be copied into the restricted operator report queue for review, including a message selected for reporting. Operational access needed to manage the service is separate from ordinary user visibility.

Blocking restricts contact and affected visibility in both directions and removes follow/friend relationships. Unblocking does not restore those relationships automatically. Gara cannot erase screenshots or copies another person has made outside the service.

4. Services receiving information

  • Supabase: account authentication and the database holding profile, social, route, saved-drive and reporting records, including uploaded photos currently stored with database records. The current project’s primary database is in West EU (Ireland). This does not mean every service log, subprocessor or other copy stays in the EU.
  • Resend: delivery of account verification emails, including recipient email and delivery-related information. Resend states that email content and delivery records are stored in the United States; selecting a European sending region does not move that storage to the EU. Its published terms describe transfer safeguards. Gara’s account-specific records and transfer assessment still require confirmation. See Resend’s data-processing information.
  • Mapbox: map rendering, road directions, route images, terrain and traffic lookups. Requests disclose requested map areas and/or route coordinates, along with technical connection information. Owner-only GPS rules do not mean map requests stay inside Gara’s database.
  • Apple and Google: their sign-in services when selected. iPhone location and reverse-geocoding services are also used for device positioning and location names.
  • Vercel: website hosting, receiving web requests and technical information such as IP address. The marketing homepage loads Google Fonts, which creates requests to Google; these document pages use local styles and system-font fallbacks.
  • Google Gmail: the support mailbox, processing information you choose to email.

Requests and SDKs can involve provider logs and device/network information. Provider storage regions, contracts, subprocessors, international-transfer safeguards and native SDK telemetry are still being verified. This draft does not promise EU-only storage, no tracking, no sharing or a specific security standard.

Mapbox telemetry: Mapbox documents default collection of de-identified location and usage data by its mobile SDK and requires an in-app opt-out. It says its default attribution control includes this option. Gara’s inspected map component enables attribution, but the actual opt-out and telemetry behavior still need signed-device verification. This is separate from Gara’s saved-drive visibility rules. See Mapbox’s mobile SDK guidance.

5. Purposes and legal bases — confirmation needed

The purposes above reflect the code. Daniel must assess and document the applicable GDPR legal basis for each purpose before this becomes a final policy, including any legitimate interests and balancing assessment. No basis has been selected by this draft. A device permission is not automatically GDPR consent. Final text must explain required versus optional information, consequences of not supplying it, and any consent withdrawal process.

6. Deletion and retention

The implemented account-settings flow requests recent reauthentication and calls a server deletion process for the authenticated account and linked profile-owned database records. Sign-out clears the active local session. Uninstalling the app is not account deletion. Signed-device end-to-end deletion verification is still pending.

While public sign-in is closed, or if you cannot access your account, contact gara.support@gmail.com to request deletion. Requests concerning older prototype records also require enough information to locate and verify the relevant record; new accounts cannot claim those records.

The report queue can retain submitted-content evidence and target references after account deletion; removing the reporter link does not necessarily anonymize that evidence. A lawful basis for any retained evidence still needs assessment. Provider logs, backups and device caches also require confirmed deletion/retention processes. There is no verified automatic expiry schedule for these categories in the inspected implementation.

Approved retention direction — not implemented yet

Daniel approved deleting ordinary support emails and report evidence 90 days after the case closes, subject to legal review and implementation. The intended purpose of this limited follow-up window is to handle follow-up questions or appeals. Any longer retention requires a documented, justified reason and review; approval of this direction does not itself establish a lawful basis. Case closure, expiry and any exceptions still need operational definitions and verification.

This is an approved target, not current automatic deletion behavior. Other categories, including GPS, account data, logs and backups, still require appropriate periods or meaningful criteria. Earlier applicable erasure rights are not automatically delayed by this target. Gara must not retain data indefinitely merely because the code lacks an expiry job.

7. Your privacy rights

Under applicable data-protection law, you may request information and access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability where applicable, and object to qualifying processing. If processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it without affecting earlier lawful processing. Rights have conditions and exceptions; they are not all available for every record or purpose.

Email gara.support@gmail.com. Include enough information to identify your account and request; ownership may need verification. Never send a sign-in code or password. Do not send identity documents unless a suitable secure process is specifically arranged. Requests must be handled within applicable statutory deadlines.

You can complain to Sweden’s Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten (IMY) or another competent supervisory authority.

8. Final review and changes

Gara’s approved minimum age is 18; the service is not intended for people under 18. If you believe an underage person has an account, contact gara.support@gmail.com. Age checks and the process for investigating and handling underage accounts remain to be implemented or verified; this draft does not claim ages are already verified.

Gara’s intended launch scope is worldwide; public registration is not open, and jurisdiction-specific requirements still need review. Required operator contact details, underage-account handling, provider disclosures, retention and legal bases must be finalized. No postal address is invented here. A reviewed effective date and version, and a process for communicating material changes, must replace the draft notice before final publication.
