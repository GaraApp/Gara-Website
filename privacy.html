Privacy Policy

How Gara handles your information

Updated and effective 22 November 2026. This notice describes the website and current app behavior. Having access to the app is separate from App Store availability: people who already have the app can create a verified account without an invite.

Gara is operated by Daniel Roci, Sweden, responsible for the personal information described here. Contact gara.support@gmail.com for privacy questions and requests.

Operator contact address: Sjöfrökens väg 24, 145 54 Norsborg, Sweden.

Signup uses verified email, Sign in with Apple or Google where offered in your installed app. Email-code sign-in and email/password sign-in after a password is set are supported. Eligible users complete registration after verifying their identity and agreeing to the Terms; no invitation is required. Direct Messages is not part of version 1.0. Earlier test builds offered messages, and historical records may remain. Rankings and performance screens are hidden.

1. Information and its uses

  • Account: email, verified authentication identity, account identifiers and sign-in/session information are used to authenticate you and associate records with your account. A Gara password, when set or used, is sent to Supabase Auth for authentication; Gara does not save it in profile or social records or intentionally log it. Password setup requires a fresh email verification code. Email-code sign-in remains available for recovery. Apple or Google sign-in, when offered and chosen, supplies provider identity information and an email address; Apple may supply a private relay address. Gara does not ask for your Apple, Google or Gmail password.
  • Profile and garage: display name, username, chosen profile photo, vehicle details and images, and preferences such as language, measurement units and map style support your profile and app experience.
  • Social information: posts, captions, photos, comments, likes, follows, friendship requests and relationships support the social feed. Gara retains message participants, message text and read status from conversations in earlier test builds. Notifications include activity references and read status. Records can remain even if a feature is unavailable in your build.
  • Routes and drives: route names, descriptions, surface, location, coordinates, distance, creator and audience support route discovery and navigation. Saved drives contain the route reference, date, duration, calculated speed statistics and GPS samples. These support personal history and the driving map, not public-road speed competition.
  • Support and safety: support emails, report reasons, content references and copies of reported text/photos/profile or message information support requests and abuse investigation. Blocking records support contact and visibility restrictions.

Session information, preferences and some saved items or cached data also remain on your device. This is not a promise that all local data is encrypted or stored in Keychain. Photo selection is initiated by a user action; only share images you have the right to use.

2. GPS and your profile driving map

With device location permission, Gara uses foreground location for the map, nearby discovery and navigation. Route drives record only after you deliberately start one. You can separately enable “Map drives automatically” in Settings. This requests Always location permission and uses background GPS and iPhone motion classification to detect drives, including when Gara is not open. Samples may include precise latitude/longitude, timestamp, accuracy, speed and heading. Confirmed automatic tracks are saved locally on your iPhone for your private profile Driving Map, not for rankings or public-road speed competition. This setting is off by default. It may miss trips when iOS limits background activity, after force-quitting the app, or where GPS or motion data is insufficient; it is not a guaranteed journey log.

The owner’s driving map combines saved route-run traces with local automatic-drive tracks when enabled. Another driver’s profile does not receive your raw automatic GPS trace or this private driving history. Automatic tracks currently do not sync to Gara’s server or to another device. Display limits and date filters do not delete the underlying saved drives.

You can turn automatic mapping off and erase its locally saved tracks in Gara Settings. You can also change location or photo permissions in iPhone Settings. Denying location limits location-dependent features. Revoking permission stops future permission-dependent access; it does not itself delete saved records. Uninstalling Gara removes locally stored automatic tracks but not account records held by Gara. Shared routes, route images and photos can still reveal places you visit, even when your raw trace is private.

3. Who can see information

Signed-in, non-blocked users can view profile and garage information under the current access rules. Social posts and routes have audience rules: public means visible to other authorized signed-in users, friends means accepted Gara friendships, and private routes are creator-only. Following is directional and does not grant friendship access. Newly saved routes currently default to friends; do not assume every supported audience can be selected in every screen.

Raw recorded drive GPS and the private Driving Map are owner-only under ordinary app access. Historical messages from earlier test builds remain restricted to their participants, subject to blocking; Direct Messages is unavailable in version 1.0. Reported content may be copied into the restricted operator report queue for review, including historical message evidence. Operational access needed to manage the service is separate from ordinary user visibility.

Blocking restricts contact and affected visibility in both directions and removes follow/friend relationships. Unblocking does not restore those relationships automatically. Gara cannot erase screenshots or copies another person has made outside the service.

4. Services receiving information

  • Supabase: account authentication and the database holding profile, social, route, saved-drive and reporting records, including uploaded photos currently stored with database records. The current project’s primary database is in West EU (Ireland). This does not mean every service log, subprocessor or other copy stays in the EU.
  • Resend: delivery of account verification emails, including recipient email and delivery-related information. Resend states that email content and delivery records are stored in the United States; selecting a European sending region does not move that storage to the EU. Gara’s account currently uses its Free transactional plan. Resend documents 30-day email/log retention while this plan is active and seven-day backups; these provider windows are separate from Gara’s support-mail and report-evidence retention. Its processing agreement is incorporated on signup, and its published terms describe transfer safeguards. Gara’s transfer assessment still requires confirmation. See Resend’s data-processing information.
  • Mapbox: map rendering, road directions, route images, terrain and traffic lookups. Requests disclose requested map areas and/or route coordinates, along with technical connection information. Owner-only GPS rules do not mean map requests stay inside Gara’s database.
  • Apple and Google: their sign-in services when selected. iPhone location and reverse-geocoding services are also used for device positioning and location names.
  • Vercel: website hosting, receiving web requests and technical information such as IP address. The website, including these document pages, loads Google Fonts, which creates requests to Google. System-font fallbacks are used if fonts do not load.
  • Google Gmail: the support mailbox, processing information you choose to email.

OpenAI and optional automatic content checks: Automatic filtering is currently off for real submissions; those submissions are not sent to OpenAI for this check. The configured provider for checks, when enabled, is OpenAI’s Moderation API, not Azure. Before sending the text or still photo being checked, Gara asks for your permission for that submission. Checks cover submitted posts, comments, profiles, garage entries and route text. When this gate is enabled, declining leaves the changes unpublished; accepting the Terms is not permission to send content to OpenAI.

The check sends the submitted text or photo, not your account email, sign-in credentials, raw GPS history or private messages. Gara removes hidden photo metadata first, but visible faces, number plates and other details may still identify someone. OpenAI states that API data is not used for model training by default; see its API data controls for endpoint-specific handling. This is not a guarantee that all Gara or provider technical records are absent. Checks can make mistakes. Use reporting, blocking or gara.support@gmail.com to raise a concern or request review.

Requests and SDKs can involve provider logs and device/network information. Gara does not promise EU-only storage or that disabling optional telemetry stops map, direction, email or website requests. Resend stores account-email information in the United States; other suppliers may process information outside the EEA. Provider roles, transfer arrangements and safeguards remain under review. Contact Gara for information about a particular transfer.

Mapbox telemetry: Mapbox documents default collection of de-identified location and usage data by its mobile SDK. Gara’s updated map setup requests that optional SDK telemetry be disabled before its map views mount and keeps provider attribution enabled. The installed development app loaded the map with attribution visible, and its native telemetry preference read disabled. Signed-release and network-level checks remain pending; this does not mean all provider collection stops. Map, directions and route-image requests still disclose information needed for those services. See Mapbox’s mobile SDK guidance.

5. Why Gara uses information

These purposes have different legal grounds under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). A device permission or agreement to the Terms is not blanket privacy consent.

  • Providing requested account features — contract, Article 6(1)(b): authenticating an account and providing the route, navigation, saved-history and social functions you choose, only to the extent the information is necessary for that particular function. Optional profile photos, vehicle details, uploads and sharing are not conditions for every function. Precise location is needed for location-dependent navigation and recorded drive traces, not for reading this website or contacting Support.
  • Protecting users and the service — legitimate interests, Article 6(1)(f): proportionate website and account security, abuse reports and blocking. The interests are preventing unauthorized access and harmful contact, investigating the specific content reported, and allowing review of a decision. This does not justify collecting unrelated GPS history for a report or keeping allegations indefinitely. Report-evidence minimization and retention safeguards remain subject to review.
  • Responding to statutory privacy requests — legal obligation, Article 6(1)(c): necessary processing to comply with applicable GDPR duties, including facilitating and responding to requests under Articles 12–22. This does not authorize unnecessary identity documents or indefinite retention of a request.

Not providing account-verification information prevents account sign-in. Not providing an optional upload or vehicle detail means that item will not appear. Denying location limits positioning, nearby discovery, navigation and drive recording; it does not erase older records.

Gara requests that optional Mapbox SDK telemetry be disabled; it does not use optional telemetry as a condition of access. Required map, email, hosting and sign-in requests still occur when their respective features are used. Website Google Fonts requests are separate from account features. Provider roles, transfers and minimization remain under review.

6. Deletion and retention

In the app, use Profile → Settings → Account → Delete account. The flow asks you to confirm your identity with a recent sign-in, then deletes the authenticated account and linked profile-owned database records. For an Apple-linked account, it also asks for Apple confirmation and requires successful server-side Apple authorization revocation before deleting the account. If confirmation or deletion fails, the account is not reported as deleted; retry or contact Support. Following successful server deletion, Gara signs out and attempts to clear its account-related local state, pending recordings and local automatic-drive history.

Sign-out clears the active local session, but is not account deletion. Turning automatic mapping off stops future recording through that feature; use its separate erase control to remove locally saved automatic tracks. Uninstalling the app is not deletion of records held by Gara or its providers.

If you cannot access your account or deletion does not complete, contact gara.support@gmail.com. Requests concerning older prototype records also require enough information to locate and verify the relevant record; new accounts cannot claim those records.

Account, profile, social, route and saved-drive records are kept while needed for the account and requested features, unless removed sooner or an applicable deletion request is fulfilled. There is no established automatic inactivity deadline or individual-drive deletion control. A verified account-deletion request removes linked profile-owned database records through the server process, subject to separately justified report evidence and provider copies. It does not erase copies other people made outside Gara, or promise immediate removal from every provider backup or device cache.

The restricted report queue can retain copies of selected content and target references after account deletion; removing the reporter link does not necessarily anonymize the evidence. For ordinary cases, a database closure now sets an expiry date 90 days later. No case is automatically closed or deleted: an operator must review and run cleanup. Older cases may not yet have closure dates. A longer hold needs a specific reason and finite review date. Earlier valid erasure requests are considered separately; they are not automatically delayed for 90 days.

Daniel intends to review ordinary support email and report evidence for deletion 90 days after the case closes. That mailbox routine is not automated or yet verified. Provider logs, backups and device caches have separate retention rules; Resend’s stated Free-plan email/log and backup windows are listed above, but they are not a deadline for every Gara copy. Contact gara.support@gmail.com about a specific record or deletion request.

7. Your privacy rights

Under applicable data-protection law, you may request information and access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability where applicable, and object to qualifying processing. If processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it without affecting earlier lawful processing. Rights have conditions and exceptions; they are not all available for every record or purpose.

Email gara.support@gmail.com. Include enough information to identify your account and request; ownership may need verification. Never send a sign-in code or password. Do not send identity documents unless a suitable secure process is specifically arranged. Gara will respond without undue delay and normally within one month of receiving a privacy-rights request. Where the law permits an extension for a complex request or multiple requests, Gara will explain the extension and its reason within the first month.

You can complain to Sweden’s Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten (IMY) or another competent supervisory authority.

8. Age, launch and changes

Gara’s minimum account age is 18; the service is not intended for people under 18. If you believe an underage person has an account, contact gara.support@gmail.com. A self-declaration is not age verification, and underage-account handling remains part of launch work.

Gara is intended for worldwide use; availability can vary by region and app version. Local requirements remain under review. Material changes to this notice will be posted here with a new effective date; where required, Gara will notify affected account holders or seek a new choice before the changed processing begins. Publication of this notice is not certification of legal compliance or store acceptance.
